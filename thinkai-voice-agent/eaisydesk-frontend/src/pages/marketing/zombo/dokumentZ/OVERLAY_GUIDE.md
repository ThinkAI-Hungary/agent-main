# Ultimate Marketing Overlay Design Guide — Pro Standards

This guide is the master reference for creating professional, high-conversion marketing image overlays. It covers everything from psychological color triggers to technical "laws" of design.

---

## 1. The 5 Golden Laws of Overlays

1.  **The Law of Immediacy**: If the message isn't readable in **0.5 seconds**, it fails. Contrast is king.
2.  **The Law of Focus**: Every overlay must have exactly **ONE** focal point (Headline or CTA). Never let elements compete.
3.  **The Law of 20%**: Text and graphics should cover less than **20%** of the image area. Let the photo breathe.
4.  **The Law of Safe Zones**: Keep critical content away from the edges (at least **10% padding**). Account for mobile UI overlays (IG/FB bars).
5.  **The Law of Hierarchy**: Use size, weight, and color to create a clear 1-2-3 path for the eye.

---

## 1b. Dynamic AI Enforcement (The 4 Smart Laws)

The generation engine automatically enforces these rules in real-time based on image analysis:

1.  **Law of Visibility (Luminance Inversion)**: Text color is never static. If the background behind the text is light (>65% brightness), the text flips to dark. If dark, it flips to light.
2.  **Law of Buffer (Collision Avoidance)**: Overlays must respect the "Product Subject". If a product is centered, overlays will automatically shrink or shift to the edges to ensure the brand remains visible.
3.  **Law of Proportionality (Auto-Fit)**: Long captions are automatically scaled down to fit their containers. No more overlapping or cut-off text.
4.  **Law of Integrity (Logo Guard)**: Brand logos are protected. They are forced into safe corners and can never be obscured by other overlay elements.

## 2. Anti-Patterns: What to NEVER Do

| Mistake | Why it Fails | The Fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Rainbow" Effect** | Using 3+ vivid colors. It looks cheap and amateur. | Stick to **2 main colors** + 1 accent. |
| **Micro-Text** | Text smaller than 18pt (mobile). It becomes unreadable noise. | Headlines: **60pt+**; Body: **24pt+**. |
| **Face-Blocking** | Placing text directly over a person's eyes or a product's key feature. | Use **Negative Space** (sky, wall, floor). |
| **Thin Fonts on Busy Backgrounds** | Letters "vibrate" and disappear into the texture. | Use **Bold (700+)** weights and subtle **Text Shadows**. |
| **The "Wall of Text"** | More than 10 words. Users won't read it. | Cut the copy by 50%. Use the caption for details. |
| **Center-Everything** | Boring and blocks the main subject. | Try **Asymmetric layouts** (Rule of Thirds). |

## 3. Color Harmonies & Psychological Palettes

Choosing colors isn't about "what looks nice"—it's about how you want the user to **feel**.

### A. Mood-Based Palettes
*   **LUXURY (Exclusive, High-End)**: 
    *   *Palette*: Onyx Black (#0A0A0A) + Champagne Gold (#D4AF37) + Pure White.
    *   *Rule*: Use high negative space and serif fonts (e.g., Playfair).
*   **URGENCY (Flash Sales, Limited Offers)**:
    *   *Palette*: Signal Red (#EF4444) + Bright Yellow (#FDE047) + Stark Black.
    *   *Rule*: High contrast, bold sans-serifs, and large badges.
*   **TRUST (B2B, Fintech, Healthcare)**:
    *   *Palette*: Navy Blue (#1E3A8A) + Slate Grey (#64748B) + Sky Blue.
    *   *Rule*: Professional, clean, left-aligned text.
*   **PLAYFUL (Friendly, Youthful, Creative)**:
    *   *Palette*: Mint Green (#A7F3D0) + Soft Peach (#FFEDD5) + Lavender.
    *   *Rule*: Rounded corners, soft gradients, and informal fonts.

### B. Technical Color Rules
*   **The 60-30-10 Rule**: 60% neutral background, 30% secondary brand color, 10% high-contrast accent (CTA button).
*   **Complementary Contrast**: Use colors opposite each other (Blue/Orange) only for CTAs to make them pop.
*   **The Grayscale Test**: If you can't read your design in Black & White, your contrast is too low. **Target 4.5:1 ratio.**

## 4. Master Layout Patterns

| Pattern | Layout Logic | Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The L-Shape** | Text along left and bottom edges. | Great for landscape shots with subject on right. |
| **The Central Badge** | Circle or Hexagon in the middle with minimal text. | Flash Sales, "%" discounts, Holiday specials. |
| **The Split** | 50% Image / 50% Solid color block. | Content-heavy posts (Checklists, Quotes). |
| **The Hero Header** | Giant headline at the top, minimal info below. | New product launches, "Coming Soon". |

## 5. Object Placement & Attributes

### Headline (Role: Hook)
*   **Placement**: Top or Center-Left.
*   **Attributes**: 
    *   *Weight*: 900 (Black).
    *   *Tracking*: -2% (Tighter).
    *   *Shadow*: Deep & soft (`0 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`).

### CTA (Role: Conversion)
*   **Placement**: Bottom-Center or Bottom-Right.
*   **Attributes**:
    *   *Shape*: Pill (fully rounded) or Neo-brutalist (thick black border).
    *   *Color*: High contrast (e.g., Yellow on Navy).
    *   *Animation (Mental)*: Needs to look "clickable".

### Badges (Role: Urgency)
*   **Placement**: Corner (Dog-ear) or floating circle.
*   **Attributes**: 
    *   *Contrast*: Inverted from main palette.
    *   *Detail*: Keep it to 1 word or 1 number (e.g., "SALE", "-30%").

## 6. Pro Design Checklist (Before Rendering)

- [ ] **Mobile Preview**: Shrink the design to 20% size. Is it still readable?
- [ ] **Accessibility**: Check contrast ratios for color-blind users.
- [ ] **Safe Zones**: Is the text at least 100px from any edge (on a 1080px canvas)?
- [ ] **Brand DNA**: Does the font and color match the `activeBrandKit`?
- [ ] **Subject Integrity**: Is the main product/person clearly visible and not "crowded" by text?

## 7. Strategic Combinations (Cheat Sheet)

*   **Dark Background + White Text + Gold Accent** = Premium/Luxury.
*   **Vibrant Background + Black Text + White Block** = Modern/Tech/Brutalism.
*   **Soft Image + Pastel Overlay + Serif Font** = Wellness/Beauty.
*   **Action Shot + Slanted Text + Red/Yellow** = Sports/Energy.
